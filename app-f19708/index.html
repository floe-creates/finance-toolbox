Benefits and retirement

Before taxes lowers income tax (traditional 401(k), 403(b)). Before all taxes also skips Social Security and Medicare (health, dental and vision insurance, HSA, FSA). After taxes comes out of what's left (Roth 401(k), union dues, life insurance).

These are estimates from 2026 tax tables, the same way payroll withholding is figured. Your real paycheck can differ by a few dollars. For the closest match, copy the amounts from a recent pay stub using "Fixed amount".
